# Francisco Terán

Applied Mathematics and Computing student at Universidad San Francisco de Quito (USFQ), Ecuador. I work on machine learning for text and signals: large-scale NLP, learned communication systems and probabilistic modeling.

Research assistant at the DataHub lab · Founder and chair of the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society student chapter at USFQ

### Selected work

- **[ecuador-news-nlp-pipeline](https://github.com/AgesFranciscoTeran/ecuador-news-nlp-pipeline)**: OCR and NLP pipeline that turns ~800k scanned newspaper pages (2001–2026) into a dated corpus and a vector database of 9.5M passages.
- **[autoencoders---telecommunications](https://github.com/AgesFranciscoTeran/autoencoders---telecommunications)**: When can an autoencoder compress binary signals better than classical methods? Benchmarked against Shannon bounds and optimal baselines.
- **[delphi-pipeline](https://github.com/AgesFranciscoTeran/delphi-pipeline)**: LLM-based method to analyze free-text Delphi consensus studies round by round, validated against human coding. Paper in preparation.
- **[kairos-tech](https://github.com/AgesFranciscoTeran/kairos-tech)**: Wearable that estimates physiological arousal from wrist signals and triggers local interventions.
